getting an accurate reading

  • 📐 Same plane, facing the camera. Hold your reference object upright, at roughly the same distance from the camera as the thing you're measuring.
  • 📏 Match the reference to the object. A tiny coin next to a whole human amplifies error. Use card/paper for bigger objects.
  • 🧍 Measuring a person? Tape paper to a wall beside them, or hold it at chest height, then step back so both fully fit in frame.
  • 💡 Even lighting helps. Avoid harsh dark shadows across the coin's edge.
